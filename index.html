SimpleDocs!

This is a screenshot of the SimpleDocs layout when a child is creating a document and collaborating with other students.

SimpleDocs! is a project I worked on that is aimed at young children who, for their schoolwork, must work together on documents but for whom the full functionality of Google Docs is too complicated. In this project, I worked with a group of students to create the high fidelity mockups of the application and discuss how it could be implemented in practice if we decided to go further with the project.
